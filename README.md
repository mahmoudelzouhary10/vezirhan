## About

A complete marketing and reservations website for **Vezir Han Emirgan**, a 24/7 Turkish and Ottoman restaurant on the Bosphorus in Istanbul. The site is built to do real work for the business: show the brand, present the full menu, and turn visitors into table bookings — without any backend or paid booking service.

It is fully **trilingual (Arabic · English · Turkish)** with proper right-to-left layout for Arabic, and works smoothly from phone to desktop.

---

## What it does

- **Trilingual interface** — Arabic, English, and Turkish, with correct RTL handling for Arabic typography
- **WhatsApp-based reservations** — guests pick a time, party size, and seating preference, and the booking is sent straight to the restaurant over WhatsApp (no backend needed)
- **Categorised menu** — Turkish, Arabic, seafood, and breakfast sections, with prices, plus a separate full multilingual menu page
- **Brand storytelling** — story, signature dishes, Bosphorus view, and private-events sections
- **Real photo gallery** — actual restaurant and dish photography
- **Practical info** — 24/7 opening hours, location with map directions, and direct contact links

---

## A few notes

**No backend, still functional.** Instead of a paid reservation system, bookings are assembled client-side and handed off to WhatsApp with the details pre-filled. For a single restaurant this keeps hosting free and maintenance near zero, while still capturing real reservations.

**Arabic was a first-class language, not an afterthought.** The layout flips correctly to RTL and the Arabic typography was treated with the same care as the Latin text — important for a venue whose audience spans Turkish, Arab, and international guests.
